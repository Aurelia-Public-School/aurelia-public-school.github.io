Our Educational Core

Build the Child First

Before competitive pressure and exam anxiety, a child needs deep roots in character, confidence, and foundational mastery.

📖

Knowledge & Phonics

Strong early literacy and numeracy. Through systematic synthetic phonics and a natural English-speaking culture, children develop an effortless, lifelong love for reading.

🌱

Character & Values

Honesty, respect, empathy, and humility. We believe discipline should be nurtured with genuine care rather than fear, building emotional confidence and kindness.

🤝

Habits & Cleanliness

Inspired by Japanese schooling traditions—children learn that maintaining their learning environment is a shared responsibility, instilling tidiness, order, and gratitude.

Parent-Friendly Innovation

Stress-Free Mornings with School-Provided Meals

Modern parents face an exhausting morning rush trying to prepare and pack multiple lunchboxes before school. Aurelia Public School provides wholesome, freshly prepared meal support at school.

  • No early-morning panic to plan, cook, and pack lunchboxes
  • Fresh, balanced, hygienic nutrition tailored for growing children
  • Communal dining instills table etiquette, gratitude, and mindful eating habits
  • Promotes equality and eliminates peer food comparison

Small, Attentive Cohort

Intentionally scaled for an intimate group of ~200 children in Edavannappara. Every child is known, closely observed, and supported with warmth.
