Thoughtful Innovation

A Truly Parent-Friendly School

We believe schools should partner with families to create calmer, happier homes.

Stress-Free Mornings: School-Provided Meals

Modern parents face an overwhelming morning rush trying to prepare, cook, and pack multiple snack and lunch boxes before the school bus arrives. Aurelia changes that.

We provide wholesome, nutritious, and child-appropriate meal support right here at school—ensuring fresh, hygienic nutrition every single day.

  • No early-morning panic to cook and pack lunchboxes
  • Freshly prepared, balanced, child-friendly nutrition
  • Promotes communal dining, gratitude, table manners, and self-cleaning habits
  • Fosters equality and eliminates peer food anxiety

Close Individual Support

With an initial scale limited to ~200 children, our teachers closely monitor each child's individual learning curve.

Slow-Learner Care: Continuous observation and personalized remedial follow-up ensure no child falls behind.

Leadership & Pedagogy

Grounded in 17 Years of Educational Service

Aurelia Public School is not starting from scratch. It is born from over 17 years of leadership, classroom service, and CBSE administration by our Founder (formerly Principal of Little India Public School - LIPS).

Every lesson plan, observation system, and teacher development framework is built on proven pedagogical leadership.

Teacher Quality as a Central Pillar

We invest deeply in our educators through continuous professional development (CPD) focusing on five dimensions:

  • ✓ Content Clarity: Clear, bite-sized concept delivery
  • ✓ Child Interaction: Dignity, warmth, and encouragement
  • ✓ Learner Environment: Tidy, inspiring classrooms
  • ✓ Pedagogical Style: Inquiry-driven rather than lectures
  • ✓ Learner Engagement: 100% active, confident participation
